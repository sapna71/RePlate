## What is RePlate?

Food can become surplus long before it becomes unusable. RePlate is designed to make that surplus discoverable and easier to redistribute.

Instead of relying on fragmented communication, RePlate brings the entire donation lifecycle into one platform — from listing surplus food to verifying the final handover.
